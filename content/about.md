This is a fresh website made with quartz instead of nextjs. I've been meaning to move my website over to an easier to maintain and simpler platform for some time, and my old nextjs site has sat in disrepair with several broken dependencies for about a year. Some pages are migrated over from my old website, but the intent of this site is to be a fresh start with a lightweight notebook style format.

The hope is that I'll be able to write notes and pages up quickly with Obsidian and publish pretty quickly, and not burden the website with anything too clunky.
